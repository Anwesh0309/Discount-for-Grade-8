# Discount Quest — Grade 8 Mathematics Interactive Learning Module

An interactive, pedagogical mathematics module designed for Grade 8 students to master the concept of **Discount**, marked prices, sale prices, percentage discounts, decimal multipliers, chained discounts, and sales tax.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Features

- **Phase 1: Wonder & Curiosity** — Explore Leo's Sneaker Dilemma (Shop A 40% off vs Shop B 20% + 20% off).
- **Phase 2: Interactive Story** — 8 visual story cards with pedagogical insights and mascot commentary.
- **Phase 3: Simulation Lab** — 3 interactive stations with guidance and hints:
  1. *Sale Sticker Studio* — Turn the dial and watch the price drop.
  2. *Checkout Counter* — Scan items, calculate sale prices, and assemble the receipt.
  3. *Deal Detective* — Trace stacked discounts, reverse calculations, and discount vs. tax.
- **Phase 4: Practice Phase (10 Game Worlds · 100 Questions)** — 10 themed worlds with 10 deterministic questions each:
  - World 1: *Sticker Street* (Sale Price)
  - World 2: *Savings Square* (Discount Amount)
  - World 3: *Multiplier Market* (Decimal Multipliers)
  - World 4: *Percent-Off Plaza* (Finding Discount %)
  - World 5: *Clearance Corner* (Multi-Item Cart Discounts)
  - World 6: *Torn Tag Town* (Working Backwards to Marked Price)
  - World 7: *Double Deal Dock* (Successive / Chained Discounts)
  - World 8: *Compare Canyon* (Comparing Competing Deals)
  - World 9: *Tax Tower* (Discount Combined with Sales Tax)
  - World 10: *Bargain Champion* (Mastery Challenges & Real-World Math)
  - Every question features **Listen 🔊**, **Hint 💡**, full explanations, interactive diagrams, 3 hearts, streak multipliers, and XP scoring.
- **Phase 5: Reflect & Scoreboard** — Conceptual reflection prompt, star awards, and celebration.

---

## 🎙️ Audio & Narration Pipeline

- **Zero Overlap:** Strict queue tracking and cleanup across phases, slides, stations, and questions.
- **Pre-generated MP3s:** All 395 narration phrases, prompts, and hints are pre-generated as static MP3 files for instant playback.
- **Natural Phrasing:** Mathematical formulas and currency amounts are written for natural pronunciation (`"dollars"`, `"percent"`, `"divided by"`, `"times"`, `"minus"`).
